# V16 Research Protocol — Electron-Flow Literacy in LC–MS/MS Mechanism Drawing

## Intervention
Students use canonical atom-mapped structures and a curved-electron-arrow editor.

## Required actions
- identify electron source as an atom/lone pair or bond;
- identify arrow target as an atom or bond;
- explain bond-order change;
- explain formal-charge change;
- provide product-ion and neutral-fragment formulas;
- connect the mechanism step to the V14 fragmentation-tree ledger;
- classify the evidence level.

## Primary outcome
Electron-flow mechanism score on an unseen paper-and-pencil fragmentation problem.

## Suggested rubric dimensions
1. atom/bond references are unambiguous;
2. electron source is chemically plausible;
3. arrow target is chemically plausible;
4. required simultaneous arrows are included;
5. no unexplained over-valence;
6. formal-charge changes are tracked;
7. product/neutral formulas are conserved;
8. evidence level is calibrated.

## Key limitation
V16 does not perform full reaction graph rewriting. The software validates representation grammar and starting-structure valence, while final mechanistic correctness remains a reasoning/rubric judgment.
